Available Government Schemes

Comprehensive list of central and state government agricultural schemes for farmers

Scheme / Update Level Date Summary Source
PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana
 Central Approved 16 Jul 2025 Large, district-targeted agriculture revitalization programme for 100 low-performing districts (productivity, diversification, irrigation, storage, credit; ~₹24,000 cr over 6 years). PIB
PM-KISAN — instalments & ongoing releases
 Central Multiple releases (2024–2025) Direct income support (₹6,000/yr per farmer in 3 instalments); regular instalment releases and beneficiary-status updates in 2024–25. PIB
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
 Central Updates 2023–2024 Crop-insurance scheme — had guideline/operational updates and digital reforms to speed settlements in 2024. PMFBY
Formation & Promotion of 10,000 FPOs
 Central Ongoing (2024) Large FPO-promotion programme (financial support, handholding); continued push in 2023–2025 with progress reports. PIB
Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
 Central Active (2024) Credit-linked subsidy facility for agri-infrastructure projects; continued disbursal and new projects in 2024. NABARD
ATMA (Agricultural Technology Management Agency)
 Central Updates (2024–2025) Extension / technology dissemination scheme — listed in Ministry's "Major Schemes" page with updates in 2024–25. AgriWelfare
Telangana: 'Kapas Kisan' / cotton procurement
 State (Telangana) Sep/Oct 2025 State procurement improvements for cotton (procurement center expansion, app for slots, Aadhaar-linked MSP payments). Times of India
Other national/sectoral updates
 Central 2024–2025 releases The Ministry and PIB posted multiple scheme updates (allocations, launches, budget items) during 2024–25. AgriWelfare
